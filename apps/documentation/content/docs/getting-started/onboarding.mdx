# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Open Source Orchestra! This guide will walk you through getting your subdomain and setting up your profile.

## Overview

Open Source Orchestra provides:
- **Gas-free subdomain registration** - No blockchain fees
- **Decentralized identity** - Own your data on-chain
- **Artist profiles** - Showcase your work and connect with fans

## Step 1: Get an Invite

Currently, Open Source Orchestra is invite-only to ensure quality and prevent spam.

### How to get an invite:
1. Join our [Discord Community](https://discord.gg/NxvSdp5U8r)
2. Request an invite in the `#invites` channel
3. Check your DMs for an invite code

### Invite format:
Invites are encoded URLs like:
```
https://oso-pit.vercel.app/onboarding?invite=BASE64_ENCODED_DATA
```

## Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

Open Source Orchestra uses **Porto** for a seamless Web3 experience.

### What is Porto?
- **Passwordless Web3** - No passwords or extensions required
- **Native payments** - Built for the modern web
- **Superpowers** - Buy, swap, subscribe and more
- **Seamless integration** - Works natively with Open Source Orchestra

### Setup Porto:
1. Visit [Porto](https://porto.sh/)
2. Sign in with your preferred method
3. Navigate to the onboarding page
4. Click "Connect Porto"

## Step 3: Claim Your Subdomain

Once connected, you can claim your unique subdomain.

### The Claim Process:
1. **Review your invite** - See your assigned subdomain
2. **Click "Claim Domain"** - No popup required
3. **Wait for confirmation** - Usually takes 10-30 seconds
4. **Success!** - Your subdomain is now yours

### What you get:
- A subdomain like `yourname.osopit.eth`
- Full ownership on-chain
- No gas fees
- Permanent ownership

## Step 4: Setup Your Profile

Now it's time to personalize your artist profile.

### Profile Information:

#### Bio
- Max 160 characters
- Tell people about your music/art
- Use emojis to express your style

#### Profile Picture
- Square image recommended (400x400px)
- Max file size: 4MB
- Supports JPG, PNG, GIF
- Appears as circular avatar

#### Social Links (Optional)
Connect your platforms:
- **Spotify** - Share your music
- **SoundCloud** - Upload your tracks
- **Twitch** - Stream your content
- **YouTube** - Video content
- **Twitter/X** - Social updates
- **Instagram** - Visual content

## Step 5: Explore the Platform

Your profile is now live! Here's what you can do:

### View Your Profile
- Visit `yourname.osopit.eth`
- See your profile picture and bio
- Click through to your social links

### Share Your Profile
- Copy your subdomain URL
- Share on social media
- Add to your email signature

### Update Your Profile
- Go to your profile page
- Click "Edit Profile"
- Update bio, avatar, or social links

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues:

**Invite not working:**
- Check if the invite link is complete
- Ensure you're not logged into another wallet
- Try refreshing the page

**Can't connect Porto:**
- Ensure Porto extension is installed
- Check if you're on a supported browser
- Try restarting your browser

**Domain claim failed:**
- Wait a few minutes and try again
- Check your internet connection
- Contact support in Discord

**Profile picture not uploading:**
- Check file size (must be under 4MB)
- Ensure it's a valid image format
- Try a different image file
